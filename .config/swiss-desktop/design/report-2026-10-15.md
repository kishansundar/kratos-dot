## 3. Icons

Candidates (official packages, licences read from their LICENSE files):

| Family | Licence | Drawing | Coverage of 50 functions | In Qt beside Geist 14 px (rendered) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lucide 1.57 | ISC (+ MIT for Feather-derived glyphs) | stroke 2 on 24, round ends | 50 | line 1.33 px at 16 px matches the text stem; clear in both modes |
| Tabler 3.49 | MIT | stroke 2 on 24, round | 50 | heaviest and busiest (detail in cup, bell) |
| Phosphor 2.1 regular / light | MIT | filled outlines, weights baked in | 50 | regular 1 px: lighter than the text; light reads grey |
| Carbon 11.90 | Apache-2.0 (IBM) | filled, square ends, 1 px | 43 (no Wi-Fi steps, ethernet, bluetooth-connected, mouse, speaker, memory) | most geometric; framed terminal and dense 3x3 grid; gaps |
| Iconoir 7.12 | MIT | stroke 1.5 on 24, round | 42 | 1 px, lighter; several gaps |

Excluded: Remix Icon (custom non-OSI licence), Material Symbols (rounded filled outlines; rejected earlier for
proportions).

Decision: **Lucide**, drawn with square ends and mitred joins (only the SVG cap / join attributes change; the
geometry is upstream's), line held at 1.33 px (Geist 14 px stem) at every size. Two optical sizes: 14 px in the bar
(about the height of its figures), 16 px in popovers. Measured in the bar: every glyph centred on row 19.5, the
middle of the capitals (rows 15-24); none enters the ruler band (rows 28-35).

Specification: one ink tone for live controls; the strong rule colour (>= 3:1) only when off or muted; amber only
for warnings (no internet, a plan limit past 80 %). States have their own glyphs (wifi steps and wifi-off,
bluetooth / -connected / -off, volume / -1 / -2 / -x, bell / bell-dot / bell-off, sun / moon); keep awake has no
off glyph, so it gets an ink tick under it while on. Hit areas: the bar slot (36 px tall, 14 + 2 x 12 px wide).
Every icon has a tooltip; popover rows lead with words, icons only where they add recognition (Wi-Fi strength,
player state). Tray apps keep their own icons, inside the tray popover only.

Implementation: `assets/icons/*.svg` (23 files, unmodified, pinned from jsdelivr) + LICENSE + README;
`components/Icon.qml` reads the file, replaces `currentColor`, sets the stroke for the size, and hands Qt's SVG
plugin a data URL (no network, no icon font). `Theme.icons` maps meaning to file. The drawn marks
(BarMark, SignalMark, LevelMark) and the asterisk (which read as a loading spinner) are gone.

## 7. Icons, third pass (night of 15 October)

A "Swiss correction" (commit 0337156) removed the ruler, the workspace ticks, the clock stroke and the numbered
sections, and replaced Lucide with Papirus symbolic. It was reverted the same night (63412a4) because you liked the
ruler and the workspace lines. Section 3's Lucide decision is superseded by this section.

Why Papirus failed in the real bar (live screenshot):
- It mixes drawing styles: solid blocks (the terminal, the Wi-Fi wedge, the cup) beside thin lines (Bluetooth,
  power). Three weights in one row.
- The cup was unreadable at 16 px.
- A terminal for Claude Code named where it runs, not what it is.
- A weak Wi-Fi signal changed the symbol's outline to a dot, which broke the bar's rhythm.

What was chosen (from rendered options, light and dark, in a replica of the bar):
- **Material Symbols Sharp**, weight 400, 20 px (optical size 20, grade -25 on dark), one variable font.
  Outlined and Rounded were the alternatives; Sharp's square ends match the ruler's ticks.
- Every symbol is drawn line-only, with one stroke on one grid. The solid Wi-Fi wedges (`network_wifi_*`) are
  avoided for the line arcs (`wifi`, `wifi_2_bar`, `wifi_1_bar`).
- Levels keep the outline. Wi-Fi lights its arcs in ink over the full fan drawn faint, in the bar and in the
  network popover. Volume is one speaker, because Material's quieter speakers are centred differently and would
  move. Off and muted states keep their own symbols.
- Claude Code is shown by Claude's own mark (Simple Icons path data, CC0; the mark is Anthropic's), drawn at 70 %
  of the em so its mass matches the line symbols.
- Also rendered and rejected: Adwaita (gaps: keep awake, unread bell; outline and solid mixed) and Fluent (no
  complete 16 px set; a 1 px line at 20 px). Breeze was excluded (no 16 px Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or weather symbols).

Packaging: `pkgs.material-symbols` added to `~/nixos/modules/fonts.nix`. The running system already carried it as a
dependency, so the shell works before the next `nrs`.

Verified: no QML warnings, symbol ink in rows 11-27 (clear of the ruler band), the bar in light and dark, the
network popover with a strong and a weak network, live after the swap (session-1.scope, empty warning log). The
lock screen is back to its ruler version (`backups/measure/hyprlock.conf.before-swisspass`; tonight's version in
`hyprlock.conf.swisspass-rejected`).
