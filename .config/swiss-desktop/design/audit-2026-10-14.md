# NixDesk shell audit, 14 October 2026

Scope: the Quickshell shell (`~/.config/quickshell`), its design system (`~/.config/swiss-desktop`), mango
(`~/.config/mango`), the lock screen (`~/.config/hypr/hyprlock.conf`), kitty, and the fonts module of the NixOS
configuration. Method: read every file; render each surface in a headless test compositor at 2560 x 1440 (shimmed so
it cannot touch the real session), in Light and Dark Mode; measure ink boxes on screenshots; check every number the
shell shows against its source; then render the live desktop.

## 1. Executive summary

The shell had become a well-built set of widgets that did not yet hold together as one design. Four root causes:

1. **No grid.** About 30 measures were off any grid (rows 34 px, headings 44, section labels 30 and 26, calendar
   cells 30, gaps 6, index marks 5 / 6 / 7 px), and list rows indented their content 12 px while headings did not,
   so nothing in a popover shared a left edge. The type scale had five named sizes plus four ad-hoc ones, and body
   text was 13 px (about 8.6 pt on this 109 ppi screen).
2. **Pictograms with their own proportions.** Two icon fonts in turn (Nerd Font, then Material Symbols): each glyph
   sits differently in its box, so none matched the text's cap height or stroke.
3. **Two latent defects in the live log:** a palette parse race (the shell could read a half-written theme file) and
   a binding loop in Now Playing.
4. **A misplaced tray and a crowded dashboard:** the tray either showed icons or expanded on hover and pushed the
   bar's contents; the dashboard repeated information in three uneven columns.

What changed: one 4 px grid with a 36 px line module (the bar's height); one named type scale (12 / 13 / 14 / 17 /
20 / 22 / 32 / 72); no icon font: status marks drawn on the capitals' 10 px grid; the NixDesk mark (the NixOS
snowflake's geometry in ink and red); a four-column dashboard whose lead figures stand on one baseline; a
click-to-reveal tray in a popover; translucency limited to the bar (blurred) and unfocused windows; both defects in
the log fixed at their cause.

Remaining risks: the tray popover and a live Light / Dark switch were not exercised on the live screen (you were
using it); the GPU cost of mango's blur was not measured; Claude Code's live theme switch was not observed.

## 5. Verification performed

- Every surface opened in the test session (dashboard, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, audio, session, calendar, media, tray,
  Claude Code, notifications, launcher, clipboard, wallpaper): no QML warnings after the fixes.
- Light and Dark renders of the dashboard; pixel crops of the bar's marks and the logo.
- Live: shell restarted on the new commit with an empty warning log; bar and dashboard rendered on your screen
  (dark); Now Playing followed your browser's track.
- Idle cost of the new shell: 0.05 % CPU over 20 s, 240 MiB resident (baseline 0.10 %, 247 MiB). The only
  always-running timer is a one-minute clock for Claude Code's reset countdowns; everything else runs while open.
- Not tested: the tray popover with real apps, a live Light / Dark switch, Bluetooth pairing (no device), GPU load.

## 6. Remaining work

- Open "2 apps" once and check your tray apps' menus (F-5).
- Run `nrs` when convenient (drops the now-unused Material Symbols; nothing breaks before).
- Optional: `ddcutil` for monitor brightness in the dashboard; peripheral batteries (the mouse reports one through
  hidpp) via UPower.
- Window borders are 2 px and grey (mango); a 1 px hairline would match the shell, if you want it.

## 7. Using it

- Dashboard: click the mark at the left of the bar, or Super+D.
- Light / Dark: the half-filled square in the bar, Light / Dark in the dashboard footer, or Super+Shift+T.
- Tray: click "2 apps" (or Super+Ctrl+T); Esc or a click outside closes it.
- Volume: the ten-tick scale (no number); click for the audio popover with the value and devices; right click
  mutes; scroll changes it.
- Claude Code: the asterisk (Super+Ctrl+K): reported model, context, limits with resets, and today's local estimate.
- Tailscale: the dashboard's Network column; the time it was read is under the details.
- Wallpaper: Super+W. Now playing: the track in the bar, or Super+Ctrl+M.
