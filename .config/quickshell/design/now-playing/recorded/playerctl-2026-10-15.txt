What real MPRIS players send, recorded 15 October 2026 before building Now Playing v2 (design/now-playing/README.md,
"Implementation notes"). The panel's layout and time-row choices are tested against these.

==== Zen 1.x (Firefox engine), a YouTube tab, playing ======================================================
$ playerctl -l
firefox.instance_1_81
$ playerctl -p firefox.instance_1_81 status
Playing
$ playerctl -p firefox.instance_1_81 metadata
firefox mpris:trackid             '/org/mpris/MediaPlayer2/firefox'
firefox xesam:title               I replaced Linux with OpenBSD - Here's What Happened
firefox xesam:album
firefox xesam:artist              Zei
firefox mpris:artUrl              file:///home/kishan/.config/zen/firefox-mpris/385834_56.png
firefox xesam:url                 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FWqQgJMPK20
$ playerctl -p firefox.instance_1_81 position       (then again 2 s later, still playing)
0.000006
0.000006
Also from D-Bus: CanSeek true, CanRaise true, CanGoNext / CanGoPrevious false, no Shuffle or LoopStatus properties,
Volume 1.0 (writable: a set of 0.9 read back as 0.9).
The art file is 336 x 188 (16:9, wide).
-> no mpris:length and a position that never moves: the "untimed" row ("zen doesn't report position"), the
   "video" layout. Not "live".

==== mpv 0.41 with mpv-mpris 1.3, a local mp3 with tags and cover art, playing ======================================
$ playerctl -l
mpv.instance-ROvpHiPO
$ playerctl -p mpv.instance-ROvpHiPO status
Playing
$ playerctl -p mpv.instance-ROvpHiPO metadata
mpv   mpris:trackid             '/mpv/mpris/Track/0'
mpv   xesam:contentCreated      2021-01-01T00:00:00Z
mpv   xesam:album               Master (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack)
mpv   mpris:artUrl              data:image/png;base64,iVBORw0KGgoAAAANSUhEUgAAAlgAAAJY… (600 x 600 png, cut here)
mpv   mpris:length              221000000
mpv   xesam:artist              Anirudh Ravichander
mpv   xesam:title               Vaathi Coming
mpv   xesam:url                 file:///…/media/a.mp3
$ playerctl -p mpv.instance-ROvpHiPO position      (then again 2 s later)
1.930866
3.912338
-> a length, a moving position, CanSeek: the "seekable" row; square art: the "album" layout.
(mpv also gives an endless HTTP stream a length that grows and says CanSeek: mpv cannot show the "live" row.)
